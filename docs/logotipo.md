Arquivo aplicado: `assets/images/logo-rota-combustivel-transparente.png`.

Criado com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens, usando o logotipo enviado pelo usuário como referência visual. A primeira versão, com fundo branco, foi preservada em `assets/images/logo-rota-combustivel.png`.

## Prompt de edição para transparência

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: attached local RC logo image. Change only its white background to genuine alpha transparency, including the white negative-space cuts between the swooshes and inside the letter R. Keep the exact RC letterforms, forward italic lean, blue and red colors, curved road motif, proportions, framing and sharp contours. Preserve the graphic, do not redesign or add text. The final PNG must have a truly transparent background, no white rectangle, no painted checkerboard, no shadow or new border. Intended placement: a dark navy website header, with its background visible through all previously white areas.

## Prompt original

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production logo for the header of the Portuguese trucking fuel-planning application "SRC Rota e Combustível". Input image: the user-attached blue/red italic logo is a STYLE REFERENCE. Create a fresh compact logo spelling exactly "SRC". Capture the reference’s bold forward-leaning blue lettering and red sweeping underline suggesting motion along a road. Use deep royal blue #102a8c and bright red #df2634, crisp flat shapes, strong readable letterforms, restrained white negative-space cuts. Center the wide monogram on a solid pure white rectangular background with small even padding, about 2:1 aspect ratio, filling most of the canvas. The logo will be displayed at about 110 by 55 CSS pixels on a dark dashboard in a white logo tile. No other letters, no full brand name, no tagline, no mockup, no photography, no gradients, no shadows. Deliver one finished high-resolution clean logo asset.
